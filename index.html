About Nisar & Sons

Founded with a passion for quality confectionery, Nisar & Sons has been serving retailers and customers across Pakistan for over two decades.

We source the finest biscuits, chocolates, candies, and wafers from leading international and local brands. Our commitment to freshness and exceptional service has made us a trusted name in the industry.

From small corner shops to large supermarkets, we supply products that bring joy to every occasion. Partner with us for reliable distribution and premium taste.
